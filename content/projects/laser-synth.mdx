## Overview

Modern sound synthesis offers infinite sonic possibilities, yet digital instruments often lack the physical expressiveness of their acoustic counterparts. The <H>Laser Synth</H> is a Virtual Reality (VR) instrument developed as my Bachelor's capstone project and presented at the <H>ICSC 2024 Csound Conference</H>.

Unlike VR installations that rely on generative randomness, the Laser Synth is designed for intentional, traditional performance. Through spatial tracking, it creates an interactable interface that bridges human movement with the infinite possibilities of digital audio in a live performance context.

## Instrument Control & Design

The instrument architecture revolves around three objects: a stationary central crystal and two hand-held orbs. The lasers which connect these objects represent the geometric relationship that drives the synthesis engine.

## Technical Implementation

The Laser Synth leverages the <URL text = "CsoundUnity" url = "https://github.com/rorywalsh/CsoundUnity"/> package to run <H>Csound</H> within <H>Unity</H>. This decoupling allows Unity to focus on spatial logic and visual feedback while Csound handles precise sound synthesis. 

The Csound code was prototyped in <URL text="Cabbage" url="https://cabbageaudio.com/"/>, and tactile VR interactions were implemented using the <H>Auto Hand VR</H> physics system.
